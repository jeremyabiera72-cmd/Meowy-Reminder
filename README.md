# MeowyReminder

- A Application created to track your period and your condition during period, it helps to remember the day when is your last period if you are easy to forget.
- also to know if your period is Early or Delay
- and also predicted the range of your period

## Other information about this app

- It was created for the reason that my Girl can tracked her period and help her ready and prepared for it

## Features

- Predicting Period days
- Save data's that help you
- Black and white theme, simple
